## Efficiency Metrics

### Burn Multiple

```
Burn Multiple = Net Burn / Net New ARR
```

**2025 Benchmarks:**
| Rating | Burn Multiple | Context |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| Excellent | <1.0x | Top 10% of startups |
| Great | 1.0-1.5x | Efficient growth |
| Good | 1.5-2.0x | Acceptable for early stage |
| Mediocre | 2.0-3.0x | Needs improvement |
| Poor | >3.0x | Unsustainable |

**Stage context:**
- Seed: 2.0-3.0x acceptable
- Series A: 1.5-2.5x target
- Series B+: <1.5x expected

### Magic Number

```
Magic Number = Net New ARR (Q) / S&M Spend (Prior Q)
```

**Interpretation:**
| Magic Number | Meaning | Action |
|--------------|---------|--------|
| >1.0 | Excellent | Scale aggressively |
| 0.75-1.0 | Strong | Invest more |
| 0.5-0.75 | Good | Keep optimizing |
| <0.5 | Inefficient | Fix GTM before scaling |

### Rule of 40

```
Rule of 40 = Revenue Growth Rate + Profit Margin
```

**Score interpretation:**
| Score | Rating | Typical Stage |
|-------|--------|---------------|
| >60 | Elite | High-growth or profitable |
| 40-60 | Great | Strong balance |
| 25-40 | Good | Acceptable |
| <25 | Needs work | Improve growth or efficiency |

**Examples:**
- High growth: 80% growth + (-40%) margin = 40 ✓
- Balanced: 30% growth + 10% margin = 40 ✓
- Efficient: 15% growth + 30% margin = 45 ✓

## Unit Economics

### LTV:CAC Ratio

**Formula:**
```
CAC = S&M Spend / New Customers Acquired
LTV = (ARPU × Gross Margin) / Monthly Churn Rate
LTV:CAC = LTV / CAC
```

**Benchmarks:**
| Ratio | Interpretation | Action |
|-------|----------------|--------|
| <1:1 | Losing money on every customer | Stop, fix economics |
| 1-2:1 | Unprofitable, concerning | Optimize urgently |
| 3:1 | Healthy baseline | Minimum target |
| 4-5:1 | Optimal | Sweet spot |
| >5:1 | May be under-investing | Consider scaling faster |

### CAC Payback

**Formula:**
```
CAC Payback (months) = CAC / (Monthly ARPU × Gross Margin)
```

**By ACV:**
| ACV Range | Target Payback | Maximum |
|-----------|----------------|---------|
| <$5K | <6 months | 9 months |
| $5-25K | 6-12 months | 15 months |

## SaaS-Specific Metrics

### MRR Movement Categories

```
Beginning MRR
+ New MRR (new customers)
+ Expansion MRR (upgrades, upsells)
- Contraction MRR (downgrades)
- Churned MRR (lost customers)
= Ending MRR

Net New MRR = New + Expansion - Contraction - Churned
```

### Churn Benchmarks

**Monthly Logo Churn:**
| Segment | Good | Great | Concerning |
|---------|------|-------|------------|
| SMB | <5% | <3% | >7% |
| Mid-market | <3% | <2% | >5% |
| Enterprise | <1% | <0.5% | >2% |

**Annual Churn:**
| Segment | Good | Great | Concerning |
|---------|------|-------|------------|
| SMB | <40% | <30% | >50% |
| Mid-market | <25% | <20% | >35% |
| Enterprise | <10% | <5% | >15% |

### Quick Ratio

```
SaaS Quick Ratio = (New MRR + Expansion MRR) / (Churned MRR + Contraction MRR)
```

**Benchmarks:**
| Ratio | Interpretation |
|-------|----------------|
| >4 | Excellent growth |
| 2-4 | Healthy |
| 1-2 | Concerning |
| <1 | Shrinking (more losses than gains) |
