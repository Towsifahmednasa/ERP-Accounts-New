# Burn Rate & Runway Analysis

## Core Definitions

### Burn Rate

**Gross Burn Rate:** Total monthly cash outflows (expenses only)
```
Gross Burn = All cash spent in month
           = Payroll + Rent + Software + Marketing + Everything else
```

**Net Burn Rate:** Monthly cash loss after revenue
```
Net Burn = Gross Burn - Revenue
         = Monthly Expenses - Monthly Revenue
```

**Example:**
- Monthly expenses: $150,000
- Monthly revenue: $50,000
- Gross burn: $150,000
- Net burn: $100,000

### Runway

**Definition:** How long your cash will last at current burn rate
```
Runway (months) = Cash Balance ÷ Net Burn Rate

Example:
- Cash balance: $2,000,000
- Net burn: $100,000/month
- Runway: 20 months
```

**Adjusted runway** (accounts for growth):
```
If net burn is changing, model month-by-month:

Month 1: $2,000,000 - $100,000 = $1,900,000
Month 2: $1,900,000 - $105,000 = $1,795,000
Month 3: $1,795,000 - $110,000 = $1,685,000
...continue until cash = 0
```

## 2025 Benchmarks

### Runway Expectations

| Stage | Target Runway Post-Raise | Minimum Acceptable |
|-------|--------------------------|-------------------|
| Pre-seed | 18-24 months | 12 months |
| Seed | 24-30 months | 18 months |
| Series A | 24-36 months | 18 months |
| Series B+ | 24-36 months | 18 months |

**Why longer runway matters in 2025:**
- Fundraising cycles have lengthened (median 20-25 months between rounds)
- Investors demand capital efficiency
- Market conditions less predictable
- Longer runway = stronger negotiating position

### Burn Rate Benchmarks

**Rule of thumb:** Spend no more than 1/18 to 1/24 of total funding per month

| Raise Amount | Target Monthly Burn | Max Monthly Burn |
|--------------|--------------------:|----------------:|
| $500K | $20-25K | $40K |
| $1M | $40-55K | $80K |
| $2M | $80-110K | $165K |
| $5M | $200-280K | $400K |
| $10M | $400-550K | $800K |

### Burn Multiple

**Definition:** Dollars burned per dollar of net new ARR
```
Burn Multiple = Net Burn ÷ Net New ARR

Example:
- Net burn: $300,000/quarter
- Net new ARR: $200,000/quarter
- Burn multiple: 1.5x
```

**Benchmarks:**
| Burn Multiple | Rating | Interpretation |
|---------------|--------|----------------|
| <1.0x | Excellent | Efficient growth, approaching profitability |
| 1.0-1.5x | Great | Strong efficiency |
| 1.5-2.0x | Good | Acceptable for early stage |
| 2.0-3.0x | Concerning | Need to improve efficiency |
| >3.0x | Red flag | Unsustainable, action needed |

**Industry context:**
- Top 10% of startups: <1.0x
- Median Series A: ~2.0x
- AI/deep tech often higher (justified by R&D)

## Detailed Burn Analysis

### Expense Breakdown by Function

Healthy expense ratios by stage:

| Function | Pre-seed | Seed | Series A | Growth |
|----------|----------|------|----------|--------|
| R&D | 50-70% | 40-50% | 30-40% | 20-30% |
| S&M | 15-25% | 25-35% | 35-45% | 40-50% |
| G&A | 10-20% | 10-15% | 10-15% | 8-12% |

**Interpretation:**
- Early stage → heavier R&D (building product)
- Growth stage → heavier S&M (scaling distribution)
- G&A should stay lean throughout

### Per-Employee Burn

```
Burn per Employee = Monthly Burn ÷ Headcount

Benchmark: $15,000-25,000 per employee per month (fully loaded)
```

**Components:**
- Salary + benefits: $10,000-18,000
- Office/equipment: $500-1,500
- Software/tools: $500-1,000
- Allocated overhead: $2,000-4,000

**Red flag:** Burn per employee >$30,000 without clear justification

## Runway Extension Tactics

### Quick Wins (Implement in 1-2 weeks)

1. **Audit software subscriptions**
   - Cancel unused tools
   - Consolidate redundant services
   - Downgrade oversized plans
   - Typical savings: 10-20% of software spend

2. **Renegotiate major vendors**
   - Ask for annual discounts
   - Request extended payment terms
   - Threaten to switch (if credible)
   - Typical savings: 10-30% on large contracts

3. **Tighten hiring**
   - Pause non-critical roles
   - Convert FTE plans to contractors
   - Delay start dates

4. **Accelerate collections**
   - Offer early payment discounts
   - Follow up on overdue AR
   - Require deposits on new deals

### Medium-Term Tactics (1-3 months)

1. **Reduce headcount costs**
   - Salary freezes
   - Reduce/eliminate bonuses
   - Shift to lower-cost markets (remote)
   - Part-time arrangements

2. **Marketing efficiency**
   - Cut low-ROI channels
   - Shift to organic/content
   - Pause brand spending
   - Focus on bottom-funnel only

3. **Revenue acceleration**
   - Annual upfront pricing incentives
   - Expansion focus vs. new logo
   - Price increases where possible

### Last Resort Measures

1. **Layoffs**
   - Plan carefully, execute decisively
   - Cut deep enough to only do it once
   - Target 6+ months additional runway
   
2. **Bridge financing**
   - Venture debt
   - Revenue-based financing
   - Convertible notes from existing investors
   - Extension rounds

3. **M&A / Acqui-hire**
   - If runway <6 months and can't raise
   - Better outcome than shutdown

## Monitoring & Reporting

### Weekly Burn Metrics

| Metric | Calculation | Target |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| Cash balance | Bank accounts | Track trend |
| Weekly cash out | Sum of disbursements | Stable or declining |
| Weeks of runway | Cash ÷ (4-week avg burn) | >52 weeks |

### Board-Level Metrics

Report monthly:
1. Current cash balance
2. Monthly burn rate (gross and net)
3. Runway in months
4. Burn multiple (quarterly)
5. Variance to budget
6. Major upcoming cash needs

## Warning Signals & Response

| Signal | Severity | Response |
|--------|----------|----------|
| Runway <12 months | Yellow | Start fundraising prep |
| Runway <9 months | Orange | Active fundraising or cuts |
| Runway <6 months | Red | Immediate action required |
| Burn > budget by 20%+ | Yellow | Investigate, course correct |
| Burn multiple >3x | Yellow | Efficiency review |
| Cash declining faster than forecast | Red | Emergency review |

### Decision Framework: When to Cut

```
Runway <12 months AND
  - Not actively fundraising with warm interest
  - OR fundraise unlikely to close in 6 months
  - OR burn multiple >3x

→ Implement cost reductions immediately
→ Target: Extend runway to 18+ months
```
