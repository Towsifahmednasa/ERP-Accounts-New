# Hiring Finance Help

## Role Definitions

### Bookkeeper

**Focus:** Day-to-day transaction recording and reconciliation

**Responsibilities:**
- Record all financial transactions
- Reconcile bank and credit card accounts
- Categorize expenses correctly
- Send invoices and track AR
- Process basic payroll (or support payroll provider)
- Prepare data for accountant

**Typical background:**
- Bookkeeping certification or experience
- QuickBooks/Xero proficiency
- No CPA required

**Cost:**
- Part-time/fractional: $500-1,500/month
- Full-time: $40,000-60,000/year

### Accountant / CPA

**Focus:** Financial accuracy, compliance, tax

**Responsibilities:**
- Review and correct bookkeeper work
- Prepare GAAP-compliant financial statements
- Month-end close process
- Tax planning and preparation
- Audit support
- Revenue recognition guidance

**Typical background:**
- Accounting degree
- CPA license (for tax work)
- Public accounting experience

**Cost:**
- Outsourced: $1,000-5,000/month
- Full-time: $70,000-120,000/year

### Controller

**Focus:** Financial operations and reporting

**Responsibilities:**
- Own the month-end close process
- Financial statement preparation
- Budget development and monitoring
- Internal controls design
- Team management (bookkeeper, staff accountant)
- Audit coordination
- System and process improvement

**Typical background:**
- Accounting degree + CPA
- 5-10+ years experience
- Industry experience preferred

**Cost:**
- Fractional: $5,000-12,000/month
- Full-time: $120,000-180,000/year

### Fractional CFO

**Focus:** Strategic financial leadership

**Responsibilities:**
- Financial strategy and planning
- Cash flow management and forecasting
- Fundraising support (models, due diligence)
- Board reporting and investor relations
- Unit economics analysis
- Strategic decision support
- Finance team hiring and oversight

**Typical background:**
- MBA or CPA (often both)
- 15+ years experience
- Startup/VC experience ideal
- Executive presence

**Cost:**
- Part-time: $3,000-10,000/month
- Interim full-time: $15,000-25,000/month

### Full-Time CFO

**Focus:** Complete financial leadership

**Responsibilities:**
- All fractional CFO responsibilities, plus:
- Build and lead finance team
- Strategic planning and M&A
- Capital markets relationships
- Executive team member
- Board member

**Typical background:**
- MBA and/or CPA
- 15-20+ years experience
- Previous CFO roles
- Industry expertise

**Cost:**
- Salary: $200,000-400,000+
- Equity: 0.5-2.0%
- Total comp: $250,000-600,000+

## When to Hire Each Role

### Hire a Bookkeeper When:

- Transaction volume exceeds 50/month
- Founder spending >5 hours/month on books
- Books are falling behind
- Preparing for accountant review

**Timing:** Usually first finance hire, often pre-revenue

### Hire an Accountant When:

- First tax filing approaching
- Raising institutional capital
- Need GAAP-compliant financials
- Complex revenue recognition
- Multi-entity structure

**Timing:** Before first tax filing or fundraise

### Hire a Controller When:

- Revenue exceeds $1M
- Month-end close takes too long
- Need internal controls for scale
- Multiple entities or complex structure
- Preparing for audit

**Timing:** Usually $1-5M revenue or Series A+

### Hire a Fractional CFO When:

- Preparing for Series A or later
- Burn/runway concerns
- Board wants sophisticated reporting
- Complex strategic decisions
- M&A activity
- Need financial modeling expertise

**Timing:** 3-6 months before major fundraise or at inflection points

### Hire a Full-Time CFO When:

- Revenue exceeds $10M
- Post-Series B or significant scale
- Complex operations (multiple products, geographies)
- Preparing for IPO track
- Need full-time strategic partner

**Timing:** Usually $10M+ revenue or Series B+

## Decision Matrix

| Situation | Primary Hire | Supporting |
|-----------|--------------|------------|
| Pre-revenue, simple | Bookkeeper (PT) | Tax accountant (annual) |
| Pre-revenue, raising | Bookkeeper + Accountant | Fractional CFO (project) |
| Seed, <$500K revenue | Bookkeeper | Accountant (monthly) |
| Seed, $500K-1M revenue | Bookkeeper + Accountant | — |
| Series A, $1-3M revenue | Controller (fractional) | Accountant |
| Series A, $3-5M revenue | Controller | Fractional CFO |
| Series B, $5-10M revenue | Controller + Fractional CFO | — |
| Series B+, $10M+ revenue | Full finance team | Full-time CFO |

## Fractional CFO Deep Dive

### Engagement Models

**Retainer (most common):**
- Fixed monthly fee
- Set number of hours (10-30/month typical)
- Ongoing relationship
- Cost: $3,000-10,000/month

**Project-based:**
- Specific deliverable (fundraise model, due diligence)
- Fixed fee or hourly
- Defined timeline
- Cost: $5,000-30,000 per project

**Interim (full-time temporary):**
- Full-time for defined period
- Bridge to full-time hire
- Crisis or transition management
- Cost: $15,000-25,000/month

### What to Look For

**Must-haves:**
- Startup experience (ideally your stage)
- Fundraising experience (if relevant)
- Strong financial modeling skills
- Board/investor communication experience
- References from founders

**Nice-to-haves:**
- Industry experience
- Exit experience
- Network of contacts
- Team building experience

### Interview Questions

1. "Walk me through your experience with startups at our stage"
2. "How do you approach fundraising support?"
3. "What does a typical monthly engagement look like?"
4. "How do you handle board reporting?"
5. "Can you share a time you helped a company through a cash crunch?"
6. "What's your approach to financial modeling?"
7. "How do you work with existing bookkeeper/accountant?"

### Red Flags

- No startup experience (only corporate)
- Can't explain things simply
- Wants to overhaul everything immediately
- No references from founders
- Doesn't ask about your business

## Building the Finance Function

### Phase 1: Foundation (Pre-seed to Seed)

```
Founder
   │
   └── Part-time Bookkeeper
       (5-10 hrs/month)
   
   └── Tax Accountant
       (annual engagement)
```

### Phase 2: Structure (Seed to Series A)

```
Founder
   │
   ├── Bookkeeper (PT or FT)
   │
   ├── Accountant (monthly)
   │
   └── Fractional CFO (as needed)
```

### Phase 3: Scale (Series A to B)

```
Founder/CEO
   │
   └── Fractional CFO or Controller
       │
       ├── Bookkeeper/Staff Accountant
       │
       └── External Accountant (tax, audit)
```

### Phase 4: Maturity (Series B+)

```
CEO
   │
   └── CFO
       │
       ├── Controller
       │   ├── Staff Accountant
       │   └── Bookkeeper/AP
       │
       ├── FP&A Manager
       │
       └── External (Tax CPA, Auditor)
```

## Outsourcing vs. In-House

### Outsource When:

- Early stage (preserve cash)
- Variable workload
- Need specialized expertise
- Building initial processes
- Testing fit before committing

**Good for:** Bookkeeping, tax, CFO (early), audit

### In-House When:

- Consistent, high workload
- Need immediate availability
- Building institutional knowledge
- Preparing for scale
- Cost-effective at volume

**Good for:** Controller (post-Series A), CFO (post-Series B)
