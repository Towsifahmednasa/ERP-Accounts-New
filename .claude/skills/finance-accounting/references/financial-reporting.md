# Financial Reporting

## Monthly Close Process

### Timeline

| Day | Task | Owner |
|-----|------|-------|
| 1-2 | Bank/credit card reconciliation | Bookkeeper |
| 2-3 | Review/categorize all transactions | Bookkeeper |
| 3-4 | Accrue unpaid expenses | Bookkeeper/Accountant |
| 4-5 | Revenue recognition adjustments | Accountant |
| 5-6 | Payroll and benefits reconciliation | Bookkeeper |
| 6-7 | Intercompany/entity reconciliation | Accountant |
| 7-8 | Generate draft financial statements | Accountant |
| 8-9 | Review, identify issues, adjust | Controller/CFO |
| 9-10 | Final statements, variance analysis | Controller/CFO |
| 10+ | Distribute to stakeholders | CFO/Founder |

**Target:** Close books within 10 business days of month-end.

### Close Checklist

**Bank & Cash:**
- [ ] Reconcile all bank accounts
- [ ] Reconcile all credit cards
- [ ] Verify cash balance ties to bank statements
- [ ] Clear any old outstanding items

**Revenue:**
- [ ] Recognize subscription revenue correctly
- [ ] Record deferred revenue for annual contracts
- [ ] Reconcile billing system to books
- [ ] Record any refunds or credits

**Expenses:**
- [ ] Categorize all transactions
- [ ] Accrue unbilled expenses (contractors, utilities)
- [ ] Record prepaid expense amortization
- [ ] Reconcile payroll to pay stubs

**Balance Sheet:**
- [ ] Roll forward fixed assets and depreciation
- [ ] Reconcile accounts receivable to aging
- [ ] Reconcile accounts payable to vendor statements
- [ ] Verify equity accounts unchanged (unless new raise)

## Board Reporting Package

### Executive Summary (1 page)

**Key highlights:**
- One paragraph on business progress
- Major wins and challenges
- Key decisions needed from board

**Scorecard:**
| Metric | Actual | Plan | Variance |
|--------|--------|------|----------|
| Revenue | $X | $Y | +/-% |
| Net Burn | $X | $Y | +/-% |
| Runway | X mo | Y mo | +/- |
| Headcount | X | Y | +/- |

### Financial Dashboard

**Cash & Runway:**
- Current cash balance
- Monthly burn rate (3-month trailing)
- Runway in months
- Cash forecast chart

**Revenue:**
- MRR/ARR (current and growth)
- New vs. expansion vs. churned
- Pipeline coverage (if B2B)

**Unit Economics:**
- LTV:CAC ratio
- CAC payback period
- Gross margin %
- Net revenue retention

### Financial Statements

Include:
- P&L (current month + YTD)
- P&L vs. budget variance
- Balance Sheet
- Cash Flow Statement

### Appendix

- Detailed expense breakdown
- Headcount by department
- Customer metrics
- Product metrics

## Stage-Appropriate Reporting

### Pre-Seed / Early Seed

**Focus on:**
- Cash position and runway
- Simple P&L (revenue vs. expenses)
- Key milestones achieved

**Frequency:** Quarterly investor updates

**Keep simple:** Don't over-engineer reporting before you have revenue.

### Seed (Revenue <$1M)

**Focus on:**
- Full financial statements (P&L, Balance Sheet)
- Cash flow forecast
- Basic unit economics (if enough data)
- Budget vs. actual

**Frequency:** Monthly internal, quarterly to investors

### Series A ($1-5M Revenue)

**Focus on:**
- Complete financial package
- Cohort analysis and retention
- Detailed unit economics
- Department-level budgets
- Headcount planning

**Frequency:** Monthly to board and internal leadership

### Series B+ ($5M+ Revenue)

**Focus on:**
- All of above, plus
- Segment-level reporting
- Geographic breakdown (if applicable)
- Scenario modeling
- Long-term financial planning

**Frequency:** Monthly board package, weekly executive review

## Variance Analysis

### Budget vs. Actual Framework

| Category | Budget | Actual | Variance | Variance % | Explanation |
|----------|--------|--------|----------|------------|-------------|
| Revenue | $100K | $95K | -$5K | -5% | Deal slipped to next month |
| S&M | $40K | $45K | -$5K | -13% | Hired ahead of plan |
| R&D | $50K | $48K | +$2K | +4% | Contractor came under budget |
| G&A | $15K | $17K | -$2K | -13% | Legal fees for new contract |

### Variance Thresholds

| Variance % | Action |
|------------|--------|
| <5% | Note in report, no action needed |
| 5-15% | Explain in report, monitor |
| 15-25% | Detailed explanation, action plan if trend |
| >25% | Immediate review, corrective action |

## Financial Controls

### Approval Thresholds

| Expense Level | Approval Required |
|---------------|-------------------|
| <$1,000 | Department manager |
| $1,000-$10,000 | VP/Director |
| $10,000-$50,000 | CEO |
| >$50,000 | CEO + Board notification |

### Segregation of Duties

- Different people should handle:
  - Invoice approval vs. payment execution
  - Payroll setup vs. payroll approval
  - Bank reconciliation vs. transaction recording

### Documentation Requirements

- Receipts for all expenses >$75
- Contracts for all recurring services
- Approval documentation for large purchases
- Vendor W-9s on file before payment
